# Lease Desk · Approvals

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

One of four Lease Desk agents. It answers the **Lease Desk** coordinator and
no other agent, because it changes the books.

It approves or holds a charge as the person Lease Desk is configured to act
for. Lease Desk's role checks apply: a change the role can't make is refused
and recorded. Every approval goes on the audit trail. Approving a held charge
upholds the hold: only the undisputed amount is posted.

Talk to the coordinator, **lease-desk**, instead.
