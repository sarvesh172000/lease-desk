# Lease Desk · Reconciliation

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

One of four Lease Desk agents. It answers the **Lease Desk** coordinator and
no other agent.

It reads which landlord charges are on hold because they disagree with the
lease, and the evidence behind one of them: what was billed, what the lease
supports, and the clause the check rests on.

Talk to the coordinator, **lease-desk**, instead.
