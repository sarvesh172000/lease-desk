Jenil's plan in full, 500 hours, which is what we are building.
[`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) is the status board; these files hold the task detail.

| Phase | Hours | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|
| [Phase 1](PHASE_1.md) | 110 | Event-sourced platform, DB roles, gateway, ledger, first thin slice |

Phase 3 gains two groups beyond Jenil's original ten, both sponsor targets:
**group 11 SpacetimeDB** (14 h) and **group 12 Anthropic** (12 h). Both are
specified in [`../SPONSORS.md`](../SPONSORS.md). Total: **526 hours**.

## On the hours

500 hours is reachable at agent throughput, and only at agent throughput. The
measured evidence so far: Phase 1 groups 6, 7, 9 and 10 and most of group 8 —
about 44 hours by Jenil's own estimates — landed in roughly four hours of wall
clock with 174 passing tests.

Each phase file keeps its own cut order. Use it if a phase slips rather than
dropping quality across all of them.

## Order of work

Phase 1 before Phase 2 before Phase 3, with two exceptions already taken:

- **Phase 2 groups 3, 5, 8, 9 and 15** were started early because the demo
  moment needed them — the amendment, the mailbox, trust ranking and the
  remeasurement.
- **Phase 3 is sponsor-facing** and benefits from early booth conversations.
  Confirm prize criteria in hour one even though the work comes last.
