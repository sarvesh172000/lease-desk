# Lease Desk · Investigation

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

One of four Lease Desk agents. It answers the **Northbay Lease Desk** front
desk and no other agent.

When a landlord charge doesn't match the lease, it searches the mailbox for
the reason (an amendment, a side letter, a renewal) and ranks what it finds by
how much each source can be trusted. If the lease changed, Lease Desk's engine
remeasures the ASC 842 liability and proposes the adjusting entries. Nothing is
posted until a person approves, through the approvals agent.

It works as the person paired in the chat, on items they can see, and each
step is published live to SpacetimeDB as it happens.

Talk to **Northbay Lease Desk** instead.
