# Lease Desk · Approvals

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

One of four Lease Desk agents. It answers the **Northbay Lease Desk** front
desk and no other agent.

It works the queue of the person paired in the chat: what needs them this
week, a held charge as a review card, why an item is where it is, and their
answers and approvals. Every action is the same call the web app makes, as
that person, so Lease Desk's role and region checks decide it, the gateway
posts it, and the hash-chained audit trail records it — refusals included.

Talk to **Northbay Lease Desk** instead.
